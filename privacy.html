Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Nidra respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

Nidra collects only the information necessary to provide its core functionality.

This may include meditation session activity, points, leaderboard rankings, and consistency streaks. If you contact support, your email address will be used only to respond to your request.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the app, track progress, display leaderboards, maintain streaks, and respond to support inquiries. Nidra does not use data for advertising or marketing.

Data Sharing

Nidra does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect your information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

User Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data by contacting support.

Children’s Privacy

Nidra is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
