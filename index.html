Frequently Asked Questions

How does Nidra work?

You complete meditation sessions inside the app. Each completed session earns you points, which contribute to your overall ranking on the leaderboard. The more consistently you practice, the higher you rank compared to other users.

What is consistency?

Consistency is a score that tracks how many days in a row you complete at least one meditation session. Maintaining a daily streak increases your consistency score and reflects long-term discipline.
