Frequently Asked Questions

How does Nidra work?

You complete meditation sessions within the app. Each completed session earns points that contribute to your leaderboard ranking against other users.

What is consistency?

Consistency is a streak-based score that tracks how many consecutive days you complete at least one meditation session. Longer streaks result in higher consistency scores.
